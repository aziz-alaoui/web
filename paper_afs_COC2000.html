ABSTRACT

The elaboration of the extraction methods of efficient signal from input signal or/and recovering parameters for chaotic dynamical system is still a very attractive problem. This problem is like the problem of recovering a trajectory of the system from its $\epsilon$-perturbed trajectory. The purpose of this paper is to present some results on recovering PARAMETERS of chaotic dynamical systems given by piecewise linear maps from their $\epsilon$-perturbed trajectories.

Ref: M.A. Aziz Alaoui et al , Control of Oscillations and Chaos, Proc. IEEE, vol. 2, 230-233, eds. F.L. Chernousko, A.L. Fradkov, (1997).

Research Activities :

My research is in the area of Mathematics (Dynamical Systems, ODE, Nonlinear dynamics, chaos, ...)

including :
  • Boundedness and Stability of solutions of ODE
  • Bifurcations leading to chaotic behaviour
  • Chaotic dynamical systems
  • Chaotic mappings
  • Strange Attractors
  • Synchronization of chaos (and anti-synchronization)
  • Analysis and control of nonlinear dynamics and chaos
  • Ecological Modeling
  • My main research interest since 4 years is the application of Dynamical Systems (NonLinear Dynamics, Stability, Bifurcations Chaos, ...) theory to the mathematical modelling of biological populations, or ecological modelling, in particular using theoretical or numerical tools...
  • Complex systems

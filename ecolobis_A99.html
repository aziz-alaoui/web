Stability and Chaos in a `Realistic' Three species food chain

M. A. Aziz Alaoui
Depart. of Math., L.M., Le Havre University

submitted to SIAM Journ. Appl. Math., (2000).
Preprint Le Havre Univ.

ABSTRACT

A fairly realistic three-species food chain ecological model, using Leslie-Gower scheme, in which the generalist predator is assumed to reproduce mostly sexually, is considered. We estabilish and prove theorems on existence and stability of equilibria. Using intensive numerical qualitative analysis, we show that the model could exhibit chaotic dynamics for realistic parameters and state values. Transition to chaotic behavior is established via period doubling bifurcation, and some sequences of distinctive period-halving are found.

Ref: M.A. Aziz Alaoui, Preprint Le Havre Univ. . (1999).

Stability and Chaos in a `Realistic' Three species food chain

M. A. Aziz Alaoui
Depart. of Math., L.M., Le Havre University

submitted to SIAM Journ. Appl. Math., (2000).
Preprint Le Havre Univ.

ABSTRACT

A fairly realistic three-species food chain ecological model in which the generalist predator is assumed to reproduce mostly sexually is considered. We prove theorems on existence, asymptotic stability , global stability of equilbria. Intensive numerical qualitative analysis is also done. Transition to chaotic behavior is estabilished via period doubling bifurcation, and some sequences of distinctive period-halving are found.

Ref: M.A. Aziz Alaoui, Preprint Le Havre Univ. . (1999).

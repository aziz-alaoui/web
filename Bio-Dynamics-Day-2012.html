A one day workshop on Math-Bio

Description

The Lab of Mathematics of the Havre (LMAH), supported by several structures (Federation Normandy Mathematics-FR-CNRS-3335, the ISCN, the High-Normandie region, the FEDER-RISC, and by a network of researchers from Normandy, develops as a unifying theme: complex systems, in particular dynamical systems and applications to the living systems, (Brain dynamics, ...) It organizes regularly workshops on these topics. By the day of Thursday, May 10, 2012, the speakers come from different institutions: Pierre Auger (IRD Academy of Sciences), Benoit Perthame (Paris VI and IUF), Jean-Pierre Francoise (Paris VI), Michel Langlais (Bordeaux 2), ...

You are cordially invited to participate in the one-day above workshop, scheduled on 10th May, 2012, (09:30 a.m. - 06:00 p.m.). The workshop is FREE.
